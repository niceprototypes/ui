Lightbox `content`: content shown at its real size in place of an image, never scaled to the viewport; the overlay scrolls in both directions when it is larger, and a Close button (first in the tab order) closes it besides Escape and a click outside. `label` names the trigger and the overlay. The overlay fades in and out over the animation tokens (none under reduced motion). `imageUrl` is optional when `content` is given. Adds @niceprototypes/button as a peer dependency.
