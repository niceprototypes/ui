App `headerActions`: controls in the header row after the search and the docs link, before the menu button. DocsApp puts the settings there below laptop (DocsPage no longer shows them as a row there); from laptop up they stay on the page. ThemeToggle is one icon button showing the theme it switches to (a sun at night in the primary text colour, a moon by day in the muted text colour, the primary colour on hover and focus), named by its action ("Switch to light", "Switch to dark"); persistence and the first-paint bootstrap are unchanged.
