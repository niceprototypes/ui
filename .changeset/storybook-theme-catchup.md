Owns the day/night theme in the manager (no storybook-dark-mode peer) and exports DARK_MODE_EVENT, UPDATE_DARK_MODE_EVENT and THEME_STORAGE_KEY. Adds the manager reset and background stylesheets, a highlight token for the active sidebar colour, the body font from the fontFamily token, and sidebar tree lines at any depth.
