App's nav opens rows only from the reader: a row's own click or key, or a click on a page in the nav (which opens that page and keeps the rows above it open). Arriving at an address, a search pick, a scene's own navigation, back and forward, and the section in view (scroll-spy) no longer open rows; the active path shows on the nearest open row, and the drawer focuses that row when the current page's row is closed.
