ScaledPreview: a demo designed for a full screen, laid out at `docs.previewWidth` (1440px) and scaled uniformly to its column from laptop up (its box as tall as the scaled demo), inert, one button that opens the demo at real size in a Lightbox. Under the pointer and on keyboard focus it zooms by `docs.previewHoverScale` (1.02) over the fast animation duration and its transparent border takes `docs.activeColor`; no zoom under reduced motion. Below laptop the demo is shown as it is.
